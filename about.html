about us

Cooking with clarity

Butter Bean Plates is a quiet rebellion against the over-complicated. We believe in honest cooking — where ingredients speak for themselves and every plate tells a story.

Founded in 2026, we’re a small team of writers, chefs, and curious eaters who think the humble legume deserves a spotlight. Our kitchen is guided by seasonality, simplicity, and the joy of a well-made meal.

meet the team ↓

What we stand for

🌱 Seasonal

We cook with the rhythm of the land. Our recipes celebrate what’s at its peak — from spring peas to winter roots.

📖 Honest

No gimmicks, no fluff. We share techniques that work, ingredients that matter, and stories that connect.

🍽️ Delicious

Above all, food should taste extraordinary. We chase flavour with integrity — and a little butter.

Our story

2024

The idea — over a pot of braised butter beans, we realised there was a gap: thoughtful, ingredient-first writing that didn’t take itself too seriously.

2025

The kitchen — we built a test kitchen, tested 200+ recipes, and found our voice: warm, informed, and never fussy.

2026

Butter Bean Plates — we launched with a mission to make legume-forward cooking approachable, exciting, and utterly delicious.

Cookies Policy

Butter Bean Plates uses minimal, privacy-friendly cookies to improve your experience. This policy explains what cookies we use, why we use them, and how you can manage your preferences.

Last updated: 8 September 2026

Our approach: We believe in minimal tracking. We use only essential and performance cookies — no third‑party advertising, no cross‑site tracking, and absolutely no selling of your data.

🍪 Manage your cookie preferences

Choose which cookies you allow.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site remember your actions and preferences over time.

2. How we use cookies

We use cookies for the following purposes:

  • Essential: to keep the site secure and functional (e.g., remembering your cookie consent).
  • Performance & analytics: to understand how visitors interact with our recipes and content, using a privacy‑first analytics tool (no personal data shared).
  • Preferences: to remember your settings, such as font size or dark mode (if available).

3. Types of cookies we use

  • Session cookies: temporary, deleted when you close your browser.
  • Persistent cookies: remain on your device for a set period (up to 12 months) to remember your preferences.
  • First‑party cookies: set by Butter Bean Plates directly.
  • Third‑party cookies: we do not use advertising or tracking cookies from third parties.

4. Your control

You have full control over cookies. You can:

  • Adjust your browser settings to block or delete cookies.
  • Use the preference panel above to accept or reject non‑essential cookies.
  • Opt out of analytics by enabling “Do Not Track” in your browser.

For more details, visit aboutcookies.org.

5. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy occasionally. Any changes will be posted here with a revised “Last updated” date. If we make significant changes, we’ll notify you via a site banner.

Questions about cookies? Contact us at hello@butterbeanplates.com or via our contact form.

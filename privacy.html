Privacy Policy

Butter Bean Plates (“we”, “us”, “our”) respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you visit our website or interact with us.

Last updated: 8 September 2026

In brief: We collect only the data you give us, we use it to make your experience better, and we never sell it. You can ask us to delete your data anytime.

1. Information we collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Personal data you provide: name, email address, and any message content when you fill out our contact form, subscribe to our newsletter, or comment on recipes.
  • Usage data: pages visited, time spent, and interaction with our content (via cookies and similar technologies).
  • Device information: browser type, operating system, and IP address (anonymised where possible).

2. How we use your data

We use your information to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and comments.
  • Send you our newsletter and occasional updates (only if you’ve opted in).
  • Improve our website, recipes, and content based on how you interact with us.
  • Comply with legal obligations and protect our rights.

3. Cookies & tracking

We use minimal cookies to enhance your experience. These include:

  • Essential cookies: for basic site functionality (e.g., remembering your cookie preferences).
  • Analytics cookies: we use a privacy-friendly analytics tool (no cross-site tracking) to understand which recipes are most popular.

You can control cookie settings in your browser. For more details, see our Cookie Policy.

4. Data sharing & third parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We may share data with:

  • Service providers: e.g., email delivery platforms (like Substack) and analytics tools, all of which are GDPR-compliant.
  • Legal authorities: if required by law to protect our rights or the safety of our users.

5. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the data we hold about you.
  • Rectify inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Erase your personal data (“right to be forgotten”).
  • Object to processing or request restriction.
  • Data portability – receive your data in a machine-readable format.

To exercise these rights, contact us at hello@butterbeanplates.com.

6. Data security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data from loss, misuse, or unauthorised access. However, no internet transmission is 100% secure.

7. Children’s privacy

Our website is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with data, please contact us.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the revised version on this page with a new “Last updated” date. Significant changes will be notified via email or a site notice.

Questions? Email us at hello@butterbeanplates.com or use our contact form.
